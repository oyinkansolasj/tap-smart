- Rewards are in **pounds**, so **£1 of Rewards = £1 cash**. No conversion needed.
- No cap stated on the product page. Exclusions are in the separate MyRewards terms PDF.

Your figures were correct: 1% supermarkets, 0.25% supermarket petrol and everywhere else, 1–15% at partner retailers.

Source: https://www.natwest.com/current-accounts/reward-account.html

|---|---|---|

| Eligibility | Pay in **£1,250 per calendar month** | ✅ |

## 3. Revolut (RevPoints) ⚠️

Revolut's site and help centre blocked automated access (HTTP 403), so I could not confirm these on Revolut's own pages. The figures below agree across several 2026 UK sources, except where noted.

### Earn rate on card spending, by plan

| Plan | RevPoints earned | Effective return at 1p/point | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard (free) | 1 point per **£10** | 0.10% | ⚠️ |
| Plus | 1 point per **£10** (most sources) or **£5** (one source) | 0.10% or 0.20% | ⚠️ sources conflict |
| Premium | 1 point per **£4** | 0.25% | ⚠️ |
| Metal (~£14.99/mo) | 1 point per **£2** | 0.50% | ⚠️ |
| Ultra (~£55/mo) | 1 point per **£1** | 1.00% | ⚠️ (1 per £1 is on Revolut's Ultra page) |

- Rates are the **same for every spending category**. Groceries, flights and transport all earn the same.
- Exception: **Revolut Stays** bookings made in the app can earn up to **10 points per £1**.
- Only "eligible" payments earn points. The exact exclusions need to come from Revolut's terms.

### Cashback (cash, not points) ⚠️
Sources conflict here. An older Revolut help page says UK Metal/Ultra get **0.1% cashback in Europe and 1% outside Europe**. More recent 2026 guides (around August 2026) say UK cashback has been **replaced by RevPoints**. **Recommendation:** for UK users, model Revolut as **RevPoints only** until you confirm in the Revolut app.

### What a RevPoint is worth
- Revolut says RevPoints have **no fixed cash value**.
- Transfers to **Avios** (British Airways Club, Iberia, Vueling, Aer Lingus, Qatar) are at **1 RevPoint = 1 Avios**. ⚠️
- Other partners: Accor **2:1**, Emirates Skywards **2:1** (UK availability not confirmed). ⚠️
- The usual estimate is **~1p per Avios**, so **~1p per RevPoint** when converted to Avios.
- Under terms v2.3 (effective 13 April 2026), the SpareChange rate is no longer a fixed £0.02. The rate is now shown in the app. ⚠️

## 5. Points vs. cashback: making them comparable

Points are **not money**. The app should turn every reward into an estimated **pence-per-£ return** before comparing cards:

```
effective_return_% = (points_per_£ × value_per_point_in_pence)
```

| Reward currency | Default value per point | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NatWest Rewards | **100p** (it's £1 = £1) | Already cash |
| Amex Cashback | cash | Already cash (requires £3k/yr) |
| RevPoints | **1.0p** (if moved to Avios) | No fixed value; let the user change it |
| Amex Membership Rewards | **1.0p** (if moved to Avios 1:1) | Let the user change it |
| Avios | **1.0p** | Varies a lot with how they're redeemed |

**Recommendation:** treat the point value as a **user setting** with 1p as the default. Label point-based results as **"estimated value"** and cash results as **"guaranteed cash"**.

## 7. To check before building

1. **Revolut:** confirm the earn rates (especially Plus) and whether UK cashback still exists. Check in the Revolut app or the UK RevPoints terms.
2. **Amex Gold:** confirm the earn rates and £195 fee on the official product page (it didn't load fully).
3. **NatWest MyRewards:** partner offers change every two weeks, so the app will need a way to enter or update them.
4. **Merchant category codes:** issuers decide "supermarket" and "airline" from the merchant's category code. The app's categories should map to those codes.
